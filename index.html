📖 How to Play Crystal Defender

🎯 The Mission

A magical crystal lies in the center of the enchanted arena. Monsters emerge from the dark edges. If the crystal's health reaches 0, magic is lost! Survive all 5 waves to claim victory.

⚔️ Defense System

  • Magic Bolt: Click or tap anywhere to shoot magical bolts directly at approaching enemies. Rapid cooldown!
  • Build Pedestals: Click glowing stone pedestals or select a tower from the bottom shop bar to place defenses.
  • 🔮 Magic Tower (50🪙): Fires rapid arcane bolts at the nearest enemy. High damage rate.
  • ❄️ Frost Tower (75🪙): Fires freezing ice shards that deal damage and slow enemy movement by 50%.

👾 Monster Bestiary

  • Shadow Slime: 20 HP • Slow • 5 Crystal Dmg (+10 Pts & 🪙)
  • Goblin Raider: 40 HP • Fast • 10 Crystal Dmg (+20 Pts & 🪙)
  • Dark Knight: 80 HP • Heavy Armor • 20 Crystal Dmg (+40 Pts & 🪙)
  • Knight Commander (Wave 5): 250 HP • Elite Boss with aura!

⌨️ Controls & Hotkeys

Mouse / Touch: Aim & Fire Magic Bolt / Select Towers & Pedestals  |  Keys 1 & 2: Select Towers  |  P / Esc: Pause  |  M: Toggle Sound
